AptimizerFig. 01 · Site feasibility

01 · Civil × AI · 2025 – present

Aptimizer

An AI-assisted decision support system for planning and code-compliance of multi-tower residential complexes, co-founded and co-led with a four-member team as the DSCE final-year project (guide: Dr. Geetha L). It replaces slow spreadsheet-and-CAD feasibility work with a staged platform that takes a project from raw site to compliant, buildable layout.

  • GIS site analysis
  • Plot feasibility
  • FAR / FSI
  • Setbacks
  • IS 3861
  • 5-stage roadmap

10plots tested in Stage 1 · Infrathon 3rd prize

Aptimizer · Planning coreFig. 02 · Unit stacking

02 · Planning engine · 2025 – present

Unit allocation engine

The part of Aptimizer that fills the towers. 2BHK, 3BHK, 4BHK and duplex mixes are allocated against corridor, lift, staircase and parking constraints, with duplex stacking and shared common-area logic, and every layout is measured in carpet, built-up and super built-up area to IS 3861.

  • Unit mix
  • Duplex stacking
  • Lifts & stairs
  • Parking
  • Common areas
  • IS 3861

4unit types · carpet, built-up & super built-up

Academic projectFig. 03 · Haar cascade

03 · Computer vision · Academic

Real-time face & eye detection

A real-time pipeline in Python and OpenCV that finds faces, then the eyes inside each face, on a live webcam stream. Built on Haar Cascade classifiers and tuned for low-latency inference, so the boxes keep up with the person on camera.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Haar Cascades
  • Real-time video
  • Object detection
